Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026. This policy covers the OnlineChess website (chess.solankidigitaldrafts.com) and the OnlineChess apps for iPhone and Android.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. Then everything stays on your device.
  • With an account we keep your email address, the name you show to others, your chess progress and, if you chose one, a scrambled form of your password, so you can use it on other devices.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account, and everything stored with it, at any time from Profile.

Playing as a guest

Your progress (XP, ratings, games, lessons, settings) is saved only on your device, in the browser's or app's own storage. It is not sent to us. Online games against a friend pass your moves through our server to the other player; we do not keep them after the game.

With an account

You can sign up with your email address or with your Google account. When you create an account we store:

  • your email address, after you confirm it with a code we send. We use it only to sign you in and to send the codes and links you ask for. We never send newsletters or share it;
  • your name, which other players can see on the weekly challenge leaderboard;
  • your password, if you choose one, as a one-way scrypt hash (we cannot read it);
  • if you sign in with Google: Google's account identifier for you and the email address Google confirms. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or anything else;
  • one-time sign-in codes and links, stored only as hashes and valid for 15 minutes;
  • your progress: XP and rank, records against the computer, puzzle rating and history, your saved games (the moves), weak spots, finished lessons and openings, quests and achievements;
  • sign-in sessions (stored as hashes) so you stay signed in, for up to 30 days;
  • if you take part in the weekly challenge: your answers, game results, their times and your score;
  • if you report a username: who reported whom, so the report counts once; and, for a reported account, whether it is hidden from the leaderboard;
  • counts of recent failed sign-in attempts, to protect your account from password guessing.

We use this only to run OnlineChess: to sync your progress, show the leaderboard and keep the challenge fair.

Daily reminders

If you turn on reminders, we store the address your browser or phone gave us for notifications, your time zone and the hour you chose. The reminder is delivered through your browser's or phone's notification service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla). You can turn reminders off in Profile at any time.

Cookies and storage

We set one cookie, oc_session, and only when you sign in. It keeps you signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. The site also uses your device's local storage for your progress and settings.

Who helps us

Sign-in and confirmation emails are sent through Resend, which receives your email address and the email's content for delivery. Google handles "Continue with Google" under Google's privacy policy.

Network data

Traffic reaches us through Cloudflare, which protects and delivers the site and handles your IP address to do so. Our server uses IP addresses briefly, in memory, to limit abuse (for example too many sign-in attempts). We do not store them.

How long we keep data

Account data stays until you delete the account. Daily backups are kept for seven days, so deleted data is gone from them within a week. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days.

Deleting your data

Open Profile, sign in, choose Delete account and confirm with your password (or, without a password, with a code we email you). This deletes your account, email address, Google link, synced progress, reminders and challenge scores at once. You can also clear the progress saved on your device in the same step. If you cannot sign in, email us from the contact below with your username.

Children

OnlineChess is suitable for all ages, but accounts are not meant for children under 13 without a parent's help. We ask for no personal details beyond an email address and a name.

Your rights

You can see your progress in the app, take it with you (it syncs to any device you sign in on), and delete it. For any other request about your data, contact us.
