Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email preetbohra30@gmail.com. Please include your username if it is about your account, and what device you use.

Common questions

I forgot my password

Accounts have no email address, so there is no reset link. Email us from the address above with your username, and we will help you get back in.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, choose Delete account and confirm with your password. Everything stored with the account is deleted at once. See the privacy policy for details.

Someone on the leaderboard has an offensive name

Sign in, open the weekly challenge and press Report next to the name. Names reported by several players are hidden until we review them. You can also email us.

My progress is not on my other device

Progress syncs only when you are signed in to the same account on both devices. Open Profile and press Sync now.

The site looks out of date

Close every tab of the site and open it again. In a browser you can also clear the site's data, but that removes progress that is not synced to an account.
